# Make playlists play on one click

Settings › Playback sets what a click and a double-click do on every album, playlist and artist.
A rule changes it for one type. This page makes a rule so that a click plays a playlist, and an
album still opens.

## Make the rule

The Rulez card is in Max. A rule you make there works in every surface.

1. Open the Rulez card.
2. Press + to add a row.
3. At **When**, pick **You click a playlist**.
4. At **DeetsMusic does**, pick **Use**, then **Play**.

Now click a playlist in the Playlists card. It plays. Click an album in the Library. It opens.

## Choose one card

At **in**, pick a card. The rule then acts only in that card, so a playlist can play in Search and
open in the Playlists card.

## The other types

The same list has **You click an album**, **You click an artist** and a double-click row for each
type. When no rule answers, the Settings rows decide.

## Pins and Home

A pin has its own rows: right-click it, then **On Click** and **On Double Click**. Home tiles use
Settings › Home.
